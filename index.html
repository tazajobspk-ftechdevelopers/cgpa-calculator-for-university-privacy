Privacy Policy for CGPA Calculator for University

Last updated: November 24, 2026.

CGPA Calculator for University is developed by F-Tech Developers. This app works completely offline. We do not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

The app does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
